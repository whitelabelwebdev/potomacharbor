Decision-Ready Workforce Planning

AI Uncertainty Requires a New Approach to Workforce Planning

PotomacHarbor's inaugural study of 106 HR executives and senior leaders points to a new approach: shifting the focus of workforce planning from forecasting headcount to making faster, better workforce decisions, with explicit trade-offs as conditions change.
