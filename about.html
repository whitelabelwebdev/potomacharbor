About

A new approach to HR advisory

PotomacHarbor is purpose-built for the AI era: helping CHROs translate emerging technologies into measurable workforce impact.

We combine sophisticated tools with experienced advisors to provide practical, future-focused support:

Cutting edge research and toolkits

Custom advisory and board prep support

On-demand CHRO coaching

Peer roundtables and working sessions

Our flexible, non-seat-based approach is scalable and ensures both senior leaders and their teams can rapidly apply insights, drive alignment, and accelerate execution.

Designed by CPOs for CPOs

PotomacHarbor was built alongside the leaders it serves. We partnered with CPOs and CHROs to understand their most pressing AI challenges and uncover unmet needs in research and advisory support. We’re especially grateful to our charter advisors, whose insight and participation, including at our 2026 convening in Washington, DC, helped shape our foundation.
